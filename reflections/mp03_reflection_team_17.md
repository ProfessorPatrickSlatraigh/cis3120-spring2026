# MP03 Comparative Reflection — Team 17

The two industries show very different geographic patterns in their location events.

Financial Services events are mostly in smaller cities and suburbs in the eastern US. Regional banks like NKSH in Virginia and LKFN in Indiana open and close branches based on local market conditions. They close branches where customers use online banking more, and open new ones in growing areas. This is about managing costs and optimizing their existing network.

Travel and Hospitality events are fewer but different in nature. Casino companies like CZR and PENN announce new properties in entertainment and leisure destinations. These are expansion decisions driven by consumer demand, not cost-cutting. They go where people want to spend money on entertainment.

The key difference is this: Financial Services companies are shrinking and reorganizing their physical presence to save money, while Travel and Hospitality companies are expanding into new markets to make money. Banking is a mature industry managing fixed costs, while hospitality chases growth.

One limitation of this analysis is the small sample size. We only found 4 Travel and Hospitality events even at 360 days, which makes it hard to draw strong geographic conclusions. The results also only reflect companies that file formal 8-K announcements, so smaller operational changes are not captured.
